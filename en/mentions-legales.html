Privacy / 04

Analytics and resources

This website uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure aggregate traffic and technical performance. Web Analytics does not place advertising cookies, and statistics are not used to identify you personally. Technical data such as the page viewed, device type, browser, approximate country and performance measurements may be processed by Vercel.

The Like button stores a random identifier in your browser’s local storage to remember your choice without creating an account. This identifier and the liked page are stored in a Supabase database; they contain neither a name nor an email address and can be deleted by removing your Like.

The site’s fonts and icons are hosted locally, so displaying them creates no requests to Google Fonts or cdnjs. This site offers no user accounts, forms, newsletter or comment section.
